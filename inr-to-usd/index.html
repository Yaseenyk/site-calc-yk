About the INR to USD pair

People convert rupees to dollars to plan study or travel in the United States, to price overseas software and services, to compare a rupee salary with a dollar one, and to see what a remittance or an investment is worth in dollars.

Rupee amounts here are typed and shown in the Indian style, where 1,00,000 is one lakh and 1,00,00,000 is one crore. Type 100000 for one lakh. The dollar figure is the mid-market amount, before any bank or transfer fee.

Common reasons to convert INR to USD

  • Estimating tuition, rent and living costs in the United States against a rupee budget.
  • Pricing overseas subscriptions, software and online purchases billed in dollars.
  • Comparing a rupee salary or a freelance rate with a dollar one.
  • Checking what a rupee investment or a lump sum is worth in dollars.

INR to USD: the arithmetic

USD amount = INR amount x rate. To show the sum, this table uses a made-up round rate of 1 INR = 0.01 USD. It is not today's rate; the converter above loads that.

To go back, divide by the rate: $1.00 is ₹100.00 at the same example rate. The reverse rate is 1 / 0.01 = 100 INR per USD.

What converting there and back costs

A provider that takes a margin of 2% below the mid-market rate each way (an example, not any provider's rate) leaves you with less than you started with. Start with ₹1,000.00: after the first leg you hold the equivalent of ₹980.00, and after converting back ₹960.40, a loss of ₹39.60. Convert once, in the direction you need.

Why the rate you get is not the rate shown

The rate above is the mid-market rate. Banks, cards and transfer apps usually add a margin or a fee, so always compare the final amount that arrives. How exchange rates work explains the difference.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert rupees to dollars?

Divide the rupee amount by the USD to INR rate, or multiply it by the INR to USD rate. If 1 dollar is 90 rupees, then 9,000 rupees is 9,000 / 90 = 100 dollars. The converter does this with the live rate.

How many dollars is one lakh rupees?

One lakh is 100,000 rupees. Enter 100000 above to see the dollar amount at today's rate. It changes through the day because the rupee floats against the dollar.

What is the difference between INR to USD and USD to INR?

They are the same exchange rate read in opposite directions. If 1 dollar buys 90 rupees, 1 rupee buys 1 / 90, about 0.0111 dollars. Use the swap button to flip the pair.

Why does my bank give me fewer dollars?

The page shows the mid-market rate. Banks and transfer services use a rate with their margin built in, and may add a fee, so the dollars you receive are lower. Compare the final amount, not the headline rate.

Is the rate updated live?

It is loaded from a public rates feed when you open the page. These feeds publish about once a day, and the date of the rates is shown under the result.
