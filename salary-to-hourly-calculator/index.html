How to convert a salary to an hourly rate

Hourly pay = annual salary / (hours per week x weeks per year). With the standard 2,080-hour year (40 hours x 52 weeks), divide your salary by 2,080.

  1. Get the yearly figure. If you know a monthly salary, multiply by 12.
  2. Count your yearly hours. Hours per week times weeks per year: 40 x 52 = 2,080.
  3. Divide. Salary divided by yearly hours is the hourly rate.

Worked example: $60,000 / 2,080 = $28.85 an hour. That is $230.77 a day and $1,153.85 a week. Our guide to the 2,080-hour rule explains where the number comes from.

2,080 hours or 2,087 hours?

2,080 is 40 hours x 52 weeks and is the usual convention. A calendar year is 365 or 366 days, which is a little more than 52 weeks, so the US federal pay system divides annual pay by 2,087 hours instead and rounds each hourly rate to the nearest cent. The Office of Personnel Management explains that 2,087 is the average number of work hours in a calendar year over a 28-year cycle. The difference is about 0.34%, or $0.10 an hour on a $60,000 salary. If your employer states a divisor, set weeks per year to match it.

What this calculator leaves out

It gives gross pay and assumes the same hours every week. It does not model unpaid leave, bonuses or benefits, and it does not take off tax. To go the other way, use the hourly to salary calculator. For take-home pay, use the paycheck calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert salary to hourly?

Divide annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time year that is 40 x 52 = 2,080 hours, so $60,000 a year is $60,000 / 2,080 = $28.85 an hour.

How do I convert a monthly salary to hourly?

Multiply the monthly amount by 12 to get the annual figure, then divide by 2,080. $5,000 a month is $60,000 a year, or $28.85 an hour.

How much is a $50,000 salary per hour?

$24.04 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. At 45 hours a week it is $21.37.

How much is a $100,000 salary per hour?

$48.08 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or $38.46 an hour if the job regularly takes 50 hours.

What if I work more than 40 hours a week?

Set hours per week to what you really work. A $60,000 salary at 50 hours a week is $60,000 / (50 x 52) = $23.08 an hour, well below the $28.85 you get at 40 hours.

Why do some people use 2,087 hours?

The US federal pay system divides annual pay by 2,087 hours, the average number of work hours in a calendar year over a 28-year cycle, because a calendar year is 365 or 366 days, not exactly 52 weeks. 2,080 is the common convention elsewhere. The two differ by about 0.34%: on $60,000 that is $28.85 against $28.75.

Do paid holidays and vacation count as hours?

Yes. If you are paid for them, they are part of the salary, so keep weeks per year at 52. Reduce the weeks only for unpaid time off.

Is a salaried job paid for overtime?

Not automatically. A salaried job that is exempt from overtime pays the same however many hours you work. A salaried job that is not exempt is owed overtime. The guide to overtime pay explains the difference.

Is the hourly figure before tax?

Yes. It is gross pay.
