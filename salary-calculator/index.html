How the conversion works

Every conversion goes through annual pay. The calculator turns your figure into a year, then divides the year into the period you want. With a schedule of H hours a week, D days a week and W weeks a year:

FromAnnual payBack to this period
Hourlyrate x H x Wannual / (H x W)
Dailyrate x D x Wannual / (D x W)
Weeklyrate x Wannual / W
Bi-weeklyrate x W / 2annual / (W / 2)
Semi-monthlyrate x 24annual / 24
Monthlyrate x 12annual / 12
Quarterlyrate x 4annual / 4

Example: a $60,000 salary in every period

A bi-weekly check is smaller than a semi-monthly one because there are more of them. A bi-weekly schedule usually has two months a year with three paychecks; a semi-monthly schedule never does. The US Office of Personnel Management notes that there are usually 26 bi-weekly pay dates a year, but that some calendar years have 27.

What this calculator leaves out

It gives gross pay for a steady schedule. It does not include overtime, bonuses, commissions or tips, and it does not take off tax or deductions. For one conversion in detail, see the hourly to salary and salary to hourly calculators, or read how the 2,080-hour rule works. For what reaches your bank account, use the paycheck calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Multiply your hourly rate by the hours you work in a week and by the weeks you work in a year. At 40 hours and 52 weeks that is 2,080 hours, so $25 an hour is $52,000 a year.

How do I convert an annual salary to monthly pay?

Divide by 12. A $60,000 salary is $5,000.00 a month. Do not multiply by 4 weeks; a year has 52 weeks, which is about 4.33 a month.

Why is monthly pay not just four weeks of pay?

A year has 52 weeks, which is about 4.33 weeks a month, not 4. At $25 an hour, four weeks is $4,000.00, but the true monthly figure is $4,333.33.

What is the difference between bi-weekly and semi-monthly?

Bi-weekly means every two weeks, which is 26 paychecks a year. Semi-monthly means twice a month, which is 24. A $60,000 salary pays $2,307.69 per bi-weekly check and $2,500.00 per semi-monthly check. The yearly total is the same.

Why do some years have 27 pay dates?

With bi-weekly pay there are usually 26 pay dates a year, but over several years some calendar years have 27, because 52 weeks is 364 days and a year has 365 or 366. The US Office of Personnel Management notes this for federal pay.

How do I convert daily pay to annual pay?

Multiply the daily rate by the days you work in a week and by the weeks in a year. At $230.77 a day, five days a week for 52 weeks, that is $60,000.

Can I change the hours or weeks?

Yes. Edit hours per week, days per week and weeks per year. Use fewer weeks if you take unpaid time off, and fewer hours for part-time work. Paid holidays and paid leave count as worked.

Does this include overtime or bonuses?

No. It assumes the same pay every period. Use the overtime pay calculator to add hours at a higher rate, and add bonuses to the annual figure yourself.

Is this before or after tax?

Before. These are gross figures. For take-home pay after tax, use the paycheck calculator.
