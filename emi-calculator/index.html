What EMI means and how it is calculated

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment: a fixed payment made every month until the loan is repaid. For a reducing-balance loan, the formula is:

EMI = [P x R x (1 + R)^N] / [(1 + R)^N - 1]

  • P is the principal, the amount borrowed.
  • R is the monthly interest rate: the annual rate divided by 12, then by 100.
  • N is the number of monthly instalments (years x 12).

Each EMI pays the interest on the balance still owed that month, and the rest reduces the principal. Because the balance shrinks, the interest part shrinks and the principal part grows, even though the EMI stays the same. The calculator above shows this split for every year, and the full monthly table if you open it.

How to calculate EMI step by step

  1. Convert the rate. 9% a year is 9 / 12 / 100 = 0.0075 a month.
  2. Count the instalments. 5 years x 12 = 60 months.
  3. Apply the formula. For ₹10,00,000, the EMI is ₹20,758.
  4. Find the totals. ₹20,758 x 60 = ₹12,45,501 paid, so ₹2,45,501 is interest.

Flat rate and reducing balance are not the same

Some quotes, often for small or consumer loans, state a flat rate. Flat interest is charged on the full amount for the whole term, even though you repay part of it every month. A flat 9% on ₹10,00,000 for 5 years is ₹10,00,000 x 9% x 5 = ₹4,50,000 of interest, and an EMI of (₹10,00,000 + ₹4,50,000) / 60 = ₹24,167. The reducing-balance rate that gives that same EMI is about 15.7%. When you compare offers, ask which method the quoted rate uses.

What a part-prepayment does

A lump sum paid towards the principal lowers the balance that interest is charged on. On the example loan, an extra ₹1,00,000 after the 12th EMI, with the EMI kept the same, clears the loan in 54 months instead of 60. Total interest falls from ₹2,45,501 to ₹2,05,431, a saving of ₹40,070. Your loan agreement says whether a prepayment charge applies and how a prepayment is applied (shorter tenure or lower EMI).

What this leaves out

This is the standard EMI maths only. It does not include processing fees, insurance premiums some lenders bundle into the loan, GST on charges, or a floating-rate loan's rate changing mid-term. Your lender's sanction letter has the exact figures. For the loan size your income could support, see the loan eligibility calculator. For a specific loan type, use the home loan EMI or car loan EMI calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EMI formula?

EMI = [P x R x (1 + R)^N] / [(1 + R)^N - 1], where P is the loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and by 100), and N is the number of monthly instalments. It is the standard reducing-balance formula.

How do I calculate EMI for 10 lakh at 9% for 5 years?

P is 10,00,000, R is 9 / 12 / 100 = 0.0075 and N is 60. The EMI works out to ₹20,758 a month. Total payment is ₹12,45,501, of which ₹2,45,501 is interest.

Does the EMI stay the same every month?

Yes, for a fixed-rate reducing-balance loan the EMI is constant. What changes each month is the split inside it: early instalments are mostly interest and later ones are mostly principal, because interest is charged on the shrinking balance.

What is the difference between flat-rate and reducing-balance interest?

Reducing-balance interest, used by this calculator, charges interest only on the outstanding balance. Flat-rate interest charges on the original amount for the full term. A flat 9% on ₹10,00,000 for 5 years means ₹4,50,000 of interest and an EMI of ₹24,167, which is the same as a reducing-balance rate of about 15.7%.

How does a longer tenure change the EMI?

It lowers the EMI and raises the total interest. On ₹10,00,000 at 9%, 3 years gives an EMI of ₹31,800 and ₹1,44,790 of interest; 10 years gives ₹12,668 and ₹5,20,109.

How can I reduce my total interest?

A shorter tenure, a larger down payment (lower principal), a lower interest rate, or a part-prepayment during the loan each cut total interest, since each lowers either the balance interest is charged on or how long it is charged for.

How much does a part-prepayment save?

On ₹10,00,000 at 9% for 5 years, paying an extra ₹1,00,000 after the 12th EMI and keeping the EMI the same clears the loan in 54 months instead of 60 and cuts total interest from ₹2,45,501 to ₹2,05,431. Check your loan agreement for any prepayment charge first.

What happens to my EMI if the rate changes?

On a floating-rate loan a rate change can change the EMI or the tenure. Your loan agreement and your lender's notice say which. Enter the new rate and the remaining months in the calculator to see the new EMI.

Does the EMI include processing fees or insurance?

No. The calculator shows interest and principal only. Processing fees, insurance and other charges are separate, and your sanction letter lists them.
