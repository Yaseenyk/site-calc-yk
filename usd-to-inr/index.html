About the USD to INR pair

The US dollar (USD) and the Indian rupee (INR) are among the most searched currency pairs in the world, mostly because of money moving between the two countries: family remittances, salaries paid in dollars, student fees and imports.

The rupee floats against the dollar, so the rate changes through the day and the figure you see here will differ slightly from a bank's quote. Rupee amounts are shown in the Indian grouping, where 1,00,000 is one lakh and 1,00,00,000 is one crore.

Common reasons to convert USD to INR

  • Sending money to family in India, or receiving it.
  • Converting a salary or freelance payment quoted in dollars. For that, use the USD salary to INR calculator, which also gives monthly and hourly pay.
  • Estimating tuition, travel or shopping costs in dollars against a rupee budget.
  • Checking an import or export price.

USD to INR: the arithmetic

INR amount = USD amount x rate. To show the sum, this table uses a made-up round rate of 1 USD = 90 INR. It is not today's rate; the converter above loads that.

To go back, divide by the rate: ₹9,000.00 is $100.00 at the same example rate. The reverse rate is 1 / 90 = 0.011111 USD per INR.

What converting there and back costs

A provider that takes a margin of 2% below the mid-market rate each way (an example, not any provider's rate) leaves you with less than you started with. Start with $1,000.00: after the first leg you hold the equivalent of $980.00, and after converting back $960.40, a loss of $39.60. Convert once, in the direction you need.

Why the rate you get is not the rate shown

The rate above is the mid-market rate. Banks, cards and transfer apps usually add a margin or a fee, so always compare the final amount that arrives. How exchange rates work explains the difference.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert USD to INR?

Multiply the dollar amount by the USD to INR rate. If 1 dollar buys 90 rupees, then 100 dollars is 100 x 90 = 9,000 rupees. The converter above does this with the latest rate.

What is 1 dollar in rupees today?

The tile above shows today's rate, loaded live. 1 dollar to rupees is simply the rate itself.

Why is the rate different from my bank or money transfer app?

This page shows the mid-market rate, the midpoint between buying and selling prices. Banks and transfer services give you a less favourable rate or add a fee, so compare the amount that actually arrives.

Is the dollar to rupee rate fixed?

No. The rupee's value against the dollar changes continuously with the foreign exchange market.

What is a lakh and a crore?

A lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10,000,000. They are written 1,00,000 and 1,00,00,000 in Indian grouping, which is how rupee amounts appear above.
